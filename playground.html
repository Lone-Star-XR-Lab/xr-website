About the Lab

A hands-on space for immersive learning

Students, faculty, and staff use the XR Lab for classes, projects, training, demonstrations, games, and independent exploration.

XR, or Extended Reality, is an umbrella term that includes Virtual Reality (VR), Augmented Reality (AR), and Mixed Reality (MR).
